\section{Conclusion and Data Availability}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented a comprehensive data engineering and hierarchical graph formulation that transforms unstructured SEC Form 10-K disclosures into a longitudinal \textit{Network of Networks} spanning 108 Nasdaq-100 corporations (2021--2025). Grounded in a rigorous 10-table database schema, our methodology successfully parses complex corporate filings into structured micro-graph branches: product hierarchies, market competitors, dual-scope risk taxonomies, and business financial distributions.

Empirical validation demonstrates exceptional data fidelity. By enforcing textual lineage, the pipeline achieves a 99.91\% LLM grounding validity rate across nearly 20,000 extracted risk factors. By structurally isolating operating segments from corporate overhead, the graph successfully anchors \$42.4 trillion in cumulative operational revenue to narrative nodes. Crucially, our longitudinal analysis reveals that over 76\% of corporate operating segments underwent structural rewiring within the 5-year window, empirically demonstrating that network models must account for intra-firm architectural volatility rather than treating companies as static nodes. A current boundary constraint involves geographic revenue mapping: reconciling narrative product lines with sovereign revenue regions requires multi-modal matrix imputation for multinational firms reporting on a purely geographic basis, which we reserve for future topological research.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Data Availability:} To support future research in financial network science and natural language processing, the standardized 10-table relational database containing the anonymized schema matrices, edge lists, and longitudinal snapshots will be made publicly available via Zenodo [DOI pending publication]. Additionally, to ensure full methodological reproducibility, the Pydantic structured schemas, deterministic Python grounding scripts, the ``Belly Cut'' heuristic algorithms, custom bracket-counting JSON parsers, and multi-stage Llama 3.3 prompt architectures utilized in Tranche 3 will be included in the open-source repository.
